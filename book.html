Privacy

Maltings Entertainment LTD (Carbon Sports Bar // Market Place, Stowmarket, Suffolk IP14 1DG) is the data controller for bookings made through this site.

When you reserve a table, we collect your name, email, phone, party size, and your selected fixture, date, time, and venue zone. This data is processed via Tally.so, stored in Google Sheets and used to operate the booking system, with confirmation emails sent via Brevo. These services act as data processors on our behalf.

Lawful basis: contract performance (UK GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)) — required to manage and fulfil your reservation. Booking data is retained until the relevant fixture has taken place and operational reconciliation is complete, and is deleted within 30 days thereafter.

We do not currently use tracking cookies, advertising cookies, or marketing profiling. You can request access, correction, deletion, or portability of your data at any time by contacting bookings.carbon@gmail.com or calling 01449 674674. You may also raise concerns with the UK Information Commissioner at ico.org.uk.

Cookies & Storage

No tracking cookies. No advertising cookies. No third-party analytics that profile you.

The booking funnel uses two short-lived browser-storage entries:

  • sessionStorage carbon_funnel_redirect_hint — a single contextual message after the booking gate redirects you; deleted as soon as it's read.
  • localStorage carbon_match_data_cache — match-schedule cache (max 5 days), so fixtures still load if the data feed is briefly unreachable.

Both are strictly functional and don't require consent under UK PECR. Clearing your browser's storage removes them at any time.
